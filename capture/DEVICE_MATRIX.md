# Device matrix

Which tier runs on which hardware, and what accuracy it honestly delivers. Accuracy columns are generated by `python scripts/device_matrix.py` from the benchmark reports. **Real** = laser/tape-scored captures; **synthetic** = rendered captures with injected sensor error (video/photo via the oracle stand-in for the learned backend) - a pipeline check, not a real-world claim.

| Tier | Hardware | Capture app | Depth / pose source |
|---|---|---|---|
| lidar | iPhone 15 Pro / Pro Max, 16 Pro / Pro Max, 17 Pro / Pro Max; iPad Pro (2020+) | Stray Scanner (free) | LiDAR depth 256x192 + ARKit poses + intrinsics; no learned model |
| video | Any iPhone 15 or newer (incl. Pro) | built-in Camera, 1x, 4K30 or 1080p | MapAnything (Apache-2.0) depth+poses; Depth-Anything-V2 + PnP fallback |
| photo | Any iPhone 15 or newer (incl. Pro) | built-in Camera, 0.5x ultra-wide | same backend per room folder; rooms stitched by doorway matching |

## Planning targets (stated before measuring)

| Tier | Wall length | Ceiling height | Openings |
|---|---|---|---|
| LiDAR | ~1 % | < 1.5 cm | < 2 cm on 85 %+ |
| Video | < 3 % | 3-5 cm | 3-5 cm |
| Photo | < 8 % | 5-10 cm | 5-10 cm |

Known degradations: low light (video/photo blur -> widened intervals), mirrors (detected and removed), glass (LiDAR passes through; windows found from no-return rays), glossy floors (robust plane fits; floor from the histogram mode).
